# Long runs, race-pace work, fuelling and taper

Labels: **[E]** cited study · **[C]** coaching consensus · **[H]** heuristic.

## Long-run limits

- **Spike rule:** a long run ≤1.10× the longest single run of the last 30 days. Best-evidenced
  limit in this guide; past ~20 km it binds harder than "+2 km/week". [E Frandsen 2025]
- **Share of the week** can be larger at low volume (fewer runs). Linear between: [C Higdon, Pfitzinger]

| Race | Share at low volume | Share at high volume |
|---|---|---|
| Marathon | ~50% at ≤50 km/wk | ~38% at ≥90 km/wk |
| Half | ~45% at ≤35 km/wk | ~35% at ≥65 km/wk |
| 10K | ~40% at ≤25 km/wk | ~30% at ≥55 km/wk |

## Progression and caps [C]

| Race | Start from | Typical step | Cap new / some / experienced | Time cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5K | longest recent run | +1 km | 10 / 12 / 15 km | 75 min |
| 10K | longest recent run (≥6 km) | +1–1.5 km | 12 / 14 / 17 km | 90 min |
| Half | longest recent run (≥8 km) | +1.5–2 km | 18 / 20 / 23 km | 2:15 |
| Marathon | longest recent run (≥12 km) | +2 km | 30 / 32 / 35 km | 3:00 |

- 5K row: [H] extrapolated.
- Half: longest run >21 km (with >32 km/wk) went with faster times and no rise in injuries.
  Marathon: longest run <25 km went with slower times; >35 km gave no extra benefit. [E Fokkema 2020]
- Marathon: aim for 2–3 runs of 28–32 km in the specific phase. [C] Hansons caps at ~26 km and
  relies on cumulative fatigue (6 runs/wk, MP + strength days) — valid for time-limited runners. [C]
- Time cap so slow runners aren't on their feet 3.5–4 h in training; beyond ~3 h recovery cost
  rises with little added adaptation. [C, rationale H]
- Alternate harder long runs (pace finish) with plain easy ones; every 3rd–4th week is a cutback
  long run (see `load-and-progression.md`). [C]

## Race-pace work inside long runs [C Pfitzinger, Daniels]

- Marathon build: last 5 → 8 km at MP; specific phase: last 12 → 16 km at MP, or a 2-block
  structure (e.g. 3 × 5 km MP with 1 km easy). A long run with MP counts as a quality session.
- Half specific phase: last 5–8 km at HM pace. 10K: long run stays easy; quality is midweek.
- Progression runs (easy → MP over the last third) are a gentler alternative for new runners.

## Timing before the race [C]

- Marathon: last ≥28 km run ~3 weeks out; 2 weeks out 20–24 km; 1 week out 12–16 km.
- Half: last long run 10–14 days out. 10K: 7–10 days out.

## Fuelling and hydration

- Carbohydrate during exercise: up to ~60 g/h (single source) for 2–3 h efforts; ~90 g/h only
  as glucose+fructose for longer events. Around 1 h, a mouth rinse or small amounts can help;
  shorter efforts need none. [E Jeukendrup 2014]
- Daily carbs: ~5–7 g/kg on moderate training days, 6–10 g/kg on high-volume days; carb-load
  10–12 g/kg/day for 36–48 h before a marathon (or any race >90 min). [E Thomas 2016 (ACSM)]
- Train the gut: practise race fuel on long runs, starting low and building over weeks. [E Jeukendrup 2017]
- Fluid: aim to avoid >2% body-mass loss; don't drink so much that weight rises (hyponatraemia).
  Drink to thirst is a reasonable default for most amateur races. [E Sawka 2007 (ACSM); H for thirst]
- Caffeine 3–6 mg/kg ~60 min before improves endurance performance; test in training. [E Guest 2021]
- Easy long runs under ~90 min can be run without fuel; MP long runs should rehearse race fuel. [H]

## Taper

- Meta-analysis: best taper ~2 weeks (8–14 days), volume cut 41–60%, progressive/exponential,
  **keeping intensity and frequency**. [E Bosquet 2007]
- Marathon (158,000 Strava runners): a strict 3-week taper came out a median 5:32 (~2.6%) faster
  than a relaxed 1-week taper; strict beat relaxed at every taper length. Observational. [E Smyth
  & Lawlor 2021]
- Effective tapers range ~4–28 days; maintaining intensity is what preserves adaptations while
  volume drops. [E Mujika & Padilla 2003] Bigger training loads before → lean to the longer end. [H]

| Race | Length | Weekly volume as fraction of peak (race week last, race excluded) |
|---|---|---|
| 5K/10K | 1 wk (7–10 days) | ~0.6 |
| Half | 2 wk | 0.70, 0.45 |
| Marathon | 3 wk | 0.80, 0.60, 0.40 |
| Marathon, peak <50 km/wk | 2 wk | 0.70, 0.45 |

Table: [C], consistent with Bosquet's volume cut.

- Keep frequency (drop at most one run) and one quality session per week at race pace with reps
  cut 30–50%. [E Bosquet, C]
- Strength: last heavy session 10–14 days out; light only after; none in race week (`strength.md`). [H]
- Never shorten the taper to fit more building (`running-races.md`). [C]
- Feeling flat in taper week 1 is common. No fitness tests in race week; openers are short. [H]
- Race week: sleep, carb-load per above, nothing new (shoes, gels, breakfast). [H]
